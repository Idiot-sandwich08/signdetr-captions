what it is

Captions for sign language, right in your video call

Video calls are built around speech. If you communicate in American Sign Language, that leaves a real gap: hearing participants can't always follow what you're signing, and there's rarely someone available to interpret in the moment.

SignDETR Captions is a small step toward closing that gap. It's a Chrome extension that watches the video tiles on a Google Meet or Zoom call and, using a custom-trained sign detection model, overlays a live caption the instant it recognizes a sign. No interpreter, no app switching, just a caption that appears where the signing is happening.

It's built on SignDETR, a DETR-style object detection model trained from scratch on real hand-sign footage and refined iteratively with more images and more signs over time. Everything runs on your own machine, and no video ever leaves your computer.

how it works

01

Watch the call

Grabs frames from any video tile on Meet or Zoom's web client.

02

Detect the sign

Sends frames to your local SignDETR model for real-time inference.

03

Show a caption

Floats a caption under the video the moment a sign is recognized.

install

1. Unzip the download
2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome
3. Enable Developer mode (top right)
4. Click Load unpacked → select the folder
5. Join a Meet/Zoom call and start signing
⚠️ Requires a local inference server. This extension talks to a SignDETR model server running on your own machine at localhost:8000. You'll need to clone and run the SignDETR project's server separately before captions will appear.
