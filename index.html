What it is

Captions for sign language, right in your video call

Video calls are built around speech. If you communicate in American Sign Language, that leaves a real gap — hearing participants can't always follow what you're signing, and there's rarely someone available to interpret in the moment.

SignDETR Captions is a small step toward closing that gap. It's a Chrome extension that watches the video tiles on a Google Meet or Zoom call and, using a custom-trained sign detection model, overlays a live caption the instant it recognizes a sign — no interpreter, no app switching, just a caption pill that appears where the signing is happening.

It's built on SignDETR, a DETR-style object detection model trained from scratch on real hand-sign footage and refined iteratively with more images and more signs over time. Everything runs on your own machine — no video ever leaves your computer.

How it works

1

Watch the call

Grabs frames from any video tile on Meet or Zoom's web client.

2

Detect the sign

Sends frames to your local SignDETR model for real-time inference.

3

Show a caption

Floats a caption pill under the video the moment a sign is recognized.

Install

  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome.
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top right).
  4. Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder.
  5. Join a Google Meet or Zoom (web client) call and start signing.
⚠️ Requires a local inference server. This extension talks to a SignDETR model server running on your own machine at localhost:8000. You'll need to clone and run the SignDETR project's server separately before captions will appear.
